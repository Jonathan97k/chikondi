# Brand Logo Rationale & System Specification

**Status:** DRAFT — PENDING CHIKONDI'S APPROVAL

**Project:** Chikondi Official (Personal Brand Platform)

---

## 1. Design Direction: "Modern Editorial Meets Roots"
The brand identity for Chikondi is designed to reflect a dignified, warm, and professional multi-disciplinary presence across music, scriptwriting, production, and community care. Eschewing clichéd tropes, reggae flyers, and unverified religious iconography, the design system relies on refined editorial typography, generous whitespace, warm natural tones, and restrained gold accents.

## 2. Logo System Components
- **Monogram / Sigil (`logo-monogram.svg`):** An original geometric abstract mark combining a refined circular curve (representing unity and audio waves) intersecting with a root-inspired vertical anchor stroke (representing grounding and purpose). Scalable and recognizable down to 16px.
- **Primary Wordmark (`logo-primary.svg`):** The name "Chikondi" hand-crafted into clean vector paths with editorial serif proportions, elegant kerning, and balanced proportions.
- **Lockups (`logo-lockup-h.svg`, `logo-lockup-v.svg`):** Horizontal and vertical arrangements combining the monogram and wordmark for diverse layout applications.
- **Single-Color & Reversed (`logo-mono-charcoal.svg`, `logo-reversed-cream.svg`):** Optimized variants for stamps, invoices, merchandise, and dark-mode contexts.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Charcoal (`#20251F`):** Primary dark surfaces, hero backgrounds, and main typography.
- **Warm Gold (`#C9A54D`):** Accent highlights, focus markers, and brand emphasis used sparingly.
- **Natural Cream (`#E8E4D9`):** Light backgrounds and soft contrast panels.
- **Earthy Green (`#496348`):** Community and nature-inspired accents.
